# Journal

**Owner: John, alone. Claude never writes in this file** — not a fix, not a
tidy-up, not a suggested edit. If Claude thinks something belongs here, it says
so in chat and leaves it to you.

This is the unpolished one. No structure required, no audience. What you tried,
what confused you, what finally clicked, what you'd do differently. Date each
entry and otherwise write however you like.

Two reasons it earns its place:

1. **It's the only record of the journey.** `decisions.md` captures *what* you
   chose; this captures what it felt like to not know yet. That's unrecoverable
   a month later — you will genuinely forget what was hard.
2. **It's the best raw material you'll have.** The most readable part of any
   engineering writeup is the honest "I assumed X, and I was wrong" paragraph.
   Those can't be reconstructed after the fact; they can only be transcribed.
